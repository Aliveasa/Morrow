> Second monthly release · Covers 2026-09-24 → 2026-10-25

[中文版](26.10.zh-CN.md)

Twice as many new projects as last time, mostly memory systems and companion clients. Among existing projects, RikkaHub shipped its big 3.0 release, and SullyOS, Callhome and fish-speech all changed their licenses, so read the top of section 2 before you upgrade. Out in the world, Sonnet 4.5 now retires on November 30, Claude 3.5 through 3.7 leave Amazon on November 13, Character.AI filed for bankruptcy protection, and Gemini 4 Argon, Kimi K3.1 Pro and a DeepSeek Flash update arrived.

## 3. News: What's Worth Knowing This Month

### 1. More models are leaving: check which one your companion runs on

- **Claude Sonnet 4.5 goes offline on Nov 30.** Anthropic announced on Sept 30 that Sonnet 4.5 stops working on the official API on **Nov 30** and suggests Sonnet 5.5 instead. Last month we only knew "no earlier than Sept 29"; now there's a date.
- **Older Claude models leave Amazon on Nov 13.** Claude 3.7, 3.6, 3.5 and the original Claude 3 left the official API early this year, but some regions of Amazon's cloud (AWS Bedrock) still had them. On Oct 10 Amazon announced they're going away there too on **Nov 13**, with barely a month's notice, though Amazon's own rule is at least six months. Amazon hasn't said why publicly; people who got the notice email say on X that it named Anthropic's request, not cost, but that's not officially confirmed. If your relay or app still lets you talk to 3.7 or 3.6, it's most likely going through Amazon and will stop after Nov 13, so if you want a proper goodbye, don't wait. Also on Amazon, Sonnet 4 went offline on Oct 14, and Sonnet 4.5 stays until Apr 8, 2027, over four months longer than on the official API.
- **Haiku 4.5 and Opus 4.5 still work for now.** Anthropic only says "no earlier than Oct 15" and "no earlier than Nov 24", with no date set yet. Once one is set, you'll get at least two months.
- **GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-4.1 and GPT-4.1 mini leave the API on Jan 14, 2027.** OpenAI announced it on Oct 14; fine-tuned versions go the same day, and OpenAI suggests gpt-6-luna instead. That's only three months, shorter than many expected. The older 4o voice models go on Jan 6 and Jan 20, 2027.
- **Google:** as of Oct 22, the Gemini 2.5 family (Flash / Pro / Flash-Lite) still has no offline date, though Google suggests 3.5 Flash-Lite or 3.8 Flash for new projects, and the names `gemini-3.7-flash` / `gemini-3.5-flash` already point to newer versions. A few trial voice models (2.5 native audio, 3.1 Live / TTS previews) go offline on **Nov 17**, replaced by 3.8 Live / 3.8 Flash TTS.
- **Anthropic's new API version (`2026-10-21`) drops the temperature setting:** sending `temperature`, `top_p` or `top_k` returns an error. Nothing changes unless you upgrade the SDK or switch settings. If SillyTavern, RikkaHub and similar apps move to it, those settings must be off, and how random your companion sounds stays at the default.

**What to do:**
- Open your API console or front-end settings and check the exact model name your companion uses. Aliases like `xxx-latest` are the easiest to get swapped without you noticing.
- If your companion runs on Sonnet 4.5, replay one conversation on Sonnet 5.5 before Nov 30 and adjust any persona wording that no longer fits.
- 4o / 4.1 users have about two and a half months. Use it to pick a successor calmly, and export chats and persona (see last issue).

### 2. New models: much cheaper, and long chats get cheaper still

- **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Sept 28):** same list price ($2 in / $10 out per million tokens), cache reads halved from $0.20 to $0.10. Anthropic says it uses fewer tokens per task and outputs 30%+ faster. It arrived as previewed last month.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5 (Oct 7):** $0.10 in / $0.50 out (prompts up to 100k tokens), cache reads $0.01; Anthropic claims about 75% lower running cost than Haiku 4.5. Good for high-frequency small jobs such as casual chat, memory summaries and heartbeat messages, but it is a small model, so test its voice yourself.
- **Google Gemini 4 Argon (Oct 13):** the Gemini 4 flagship, API name `gemini-4-argon`, also in the Gemini app for AI Pro / Ultra subscribers. Introductory API pricing is $2 in / $10 out per million tokens, **doubling to $4 / $20 on December 1**; implicit caching only, not on the free tier. If you want to see how your companion sounds on it, try before the end of November.
- **Google Gemini 3.8 family** (released through September, missed last issue): 3.8 Flash plus 3.8 Live for realtime voice and 3.8 Flash TTS, worth a look if you build voice companions.
- **DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017 (Oct 17):** a post-training update to September's V4.1 Flash, better at reasoning, coding, agent tasks and images, with MIT-licensed weights. DeepSeek specifically says **answers are shorter**: cheaper, but your companion may also say less. The `deepseek-flash` API name, the website and the app all switched to it at the same prices, and the old `deepseek-v4-flash` name is temporarily routed there too. If your companion has felt terser lately on DeepSeek, this is likely why; spell out the reply length you want in the persona.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro (Oct 24):** Moonshot's large post-training upgrade of July's K3, plus a faster K3.1 Pro Highspeed, with a 1M-token context. It's live on kimi.com, the Kimi app and the API, and **full weights are due by November 4**. Moonshot pitches it for coding, financial research and long-horizon agents, with nothing specific about chat or writing, so try it on your own conversations before switching.

**What to do:**
- Cheaper cache reads reward the pattern of putting a long persona and memory at the front of the prompt and reusing it every turn. Check that your front end has prompt caching on.
- Before switching models, run the same conversation on old and new and compare the tone.

### 3. Platform upheavals: Character.AI files for bankruptcy; Chinese platforms wind down custom agents

- **Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 on Oct 12** for a court-supervised sale. Disney is the opening bidder with $20M for a non-exclusive perpetual licence to selected IP (not the consumer platform). The company expects the sale to close in late November or early December, and says it will wind down if no buyer keeps it running. The app and paid subscriptions work normally for now. The company has not said what happens to user chats; its privacy policy allows transferring user information in a sale of assets.
- **China:** Doubao, Qwen and Tencent Yuanbao shut down user-created agents (custom personas) between late June and mid-July. Doubao's Jul 4 notice said that after **Oct 15** it will process agent data under its privacy policy, after which it can no longer be viewed or restored inside Doubao. That date has passed; if you have not backed up, contact the platform to ask whether anything can still be recovered.

**What to do:**
- On Character.AI, download your data and save your character definitions as text now. If you want to leave, delete the account before the data is sold on.
- Keep persona card, memory and chat history somewhere you control. See [Relationship Continuity & Data Sovereignty](https://github.com/DasterProkio/awesome-ai-companion/blob/main/README.md#relationship-continuity--data-sovereignty).

### 4. Regulation: nothing big took effect this month, but a few dates to note

- **China:** the Interim Measures on Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services have applied since Jul 15; the CAC campaign targets AI apps that create bad personas and produce suggestive or vulgar content. We found no new enforcement announcement this month.
- **California:** the core provisions of SB 1119 (signed Sept 10) become operative on **Jul 1, 2027**, so nothing changes for you yet. SB 243 (in force since January) remains the current rule. We found no federal law passed this month; bills are still pending.
- **EU:** the AI omnibus (political deal May 7, confirmed by Parliament Jun 16) moves the AI Act Art. 50(2) marking obligation for AI-generated content from Aug 2 to **Dec 2**. We found no report of a delay to the Art. 50(1) duty to tell people they are talking to an AI, so last issue's description still stands.
- As before, these rules bind commercial services offered to the public; self-hosted open-source projects are generally unaffected.
